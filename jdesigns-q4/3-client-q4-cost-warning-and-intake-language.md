# Client Q4 Cost Warning and Intake Requirement

## Accuracy note (read first)
You said ads "can increase two to three times." The 2025 data I found does not
fully support a blanket 2-3x. Documented peak-week increases were about 40-60%
over October (about 25% over the annual average for the whole quarter), and 2-3x
shows up in claims about single peak days. Writing "up to 2-3x" into a signed
document could be called an exaggeration later. The wording below uses a
safer version that still protects you. Change the numbers if your own clients'
data shows more.

## 1. Intake / agreement clause (put in the requirements)

> **Seasonal advertising costs.** Meta sets advertising prices through a live
> auction, and prices rise sharply in Q4 (roughly mid-October through December),
> especially around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Cost per thousand impressions
> commonly runs about 25-60% above a typical month during peak weeks, and
> individual days or crowded industries can run higher. JDesigns Strategist does
> not set or control these costs. By proceeding, you acknowledge that results and
> cost per result may vary during this period, and that increasing ad budget,
> adjusting the campaign length, or accepting reduced reach are your options for
> managing it. Management fees are separate from, and not affected by, ad spend.

(Last sentence: delete if your fee structure differs.)

## 2. One-time warning message (send before Oct 15 or at onboarding if later)

**Subject: Heads up on ad costs this season**

Hi [Name],

Quick note before the holiday season starts. Every fall, the cost to run ads on
Facebook and Instagram goes up because more businesses compete for the same
shoppers. Last year, costs in peak weeks ran roughly 25-60% above normal, with
the highest prices around Black Friday and Cyber Monday.

This is set by Meta's ad auction, not by me or by anything in your campaign.
What it means for you: if your budget stays the same, your ads will reach fewer
people than in a normal month.

Options:
1. Increase budget for November-December.
2. Keep the budget and accept lower reach.
3. Pause during the peak and restart in January, when costs usually drop.

Let me know which you prefer by [date], and I will set it up. Happy to talk it
through.

[Your name]

## 3. Reply for when a client reaches out mid-season

Hi [Name], thanks for asking. Ad costs are higher right now because it is peak
holiday season, and many businesses are bidding for the same audience. That is
set by Meta's auction, not something I can change. Your ads are still running
the way we planned. I am watching [cost per result / sales] and will tell you
if anything needs adjusting. If you want, we can discuss raising the budget or
changing the schedule.
